Common questions

My database doesn't appear in the list

If you used Connect to Notion, Habit Tracker can only see the pages and databases you share with it. To share your work log:

  1. Open the database in Notion.
  2. Click ••• at the top right, then More.
  3. In the Connections group, click Add connections.
  4. Search for Habit Tracker and select it.
  5. In the app, refresh the database list.

You can also click Connect again in the app and choose the database while approving access.

If you pasted an internal integration token, follow the same steps but choose your own integration's name. If you pasted a personal access token, no sharing is needed: the app sees every database you can see in Notion, so check that you can open the database yourself, in the same workspace.

The app asks me to reconnect

Your access was removed in Notion, or it could no longer be renewed. Click Reconnect to Notion. Your local data is kept while you do.

I connected with a pasted token and it stopped working

The token has expired or been revoked. Personal access tokens expire after the period you chose when you created them (7, 30, 90 or 180 days, or 1 year, which is the default). Create a new token under Personal access tokens in Notion's developer portal, then paste it in the app under Settings → Advanced.

How do I remove Habit Tracker's access?

Click Disconnect in the app, or remove Habit Tracker in Notion under Settings → Connections. Either one ends the app's access.

Where is my data?

On your computer, in %APPDATA%\HabitTracker. See the privacy policy for details.

Is the sign-in service up?

Check its status. If it's down, the app keeps working with your local data, and you can still connect with a pasted token.
