Common questions

My database doesn't appear in the list

Habit Tracker can only see the pages and databases you share with it. To share your work log:

  1. Open the database in Notion.
  2. Click ••• at the top right, then Connections.
  3. Find Habit Tracker and confirm.
  4. In the app, refresh the database list.

You can also click Connect again in the app and choose the database while approving access.

The app asks me to reconnect

Your access was removed in Notion, or it could no longer be renewed. Click Reconnect to Notion. Your local data is kept while you do.

I connected with a pasted token and it stopped working

The token has expired or been revoked. Personal access tokens expire on the date you chose when you created them. Create a new token in Notion's developer portal, then paste it in the app under Settings → Advanced.

How do I remove Habit Tracker's access?

Click Disconnect in the app, or remove Habit Tracker in Notion under Settings → Connections. Either one ends the app's access.

Where is my data?

On your computer, in %APPDATA%\HabitTracker. See the privacy policy for details.

Is the sign-in service up?

Check its status. If it's down, the app keeps working with your local data, and you can still connect with a pasted token.
