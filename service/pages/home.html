Habit Tracker

A Windows desktop app that reads your Notion work log and turns your work sessions into habit-tracking charts: daily totals, weekly trends, streaks and a calendar heatmap.

How it works

  • You log work sessions in a Notion database, with a start and an end time for each one.
  • Habit Tracker reads that one database, keeps a copy on your computer, and draws the charts from the copy. It works offline, and your data stays on your computer.
  • It only ever reads from Notion. It can't create, change or delete anything in your workspace.

About this site

This site is Habit Tracker's sign-in helper. When you click Connect to Notion in the app, Notion sends you here for a moment so the app can receive access to the database you chose, without the app ever holding Habit Tracker's private Notion credentials. This site stores nothing about you.
